# Right sidebar and preferences

[Documentation](README.md) · [Developer README](../README.md)

The Model inspector has independent, collapsible sections. By default, Properties comes first and Outliner second, both expanded; all other sections start collapsed. Each area scrolls independently when its sections exceed its height. The current section's header stays at the top until the next section pushes it away; pinned headers can still collapse or drag their sections.

Router is a subsection of Properties, displayed only when an edge is selected. Clicking an edge reveals it, including edges in groups and ungrouped geometry. With Router active, clicking an edge selects it and switches to the pointer tool while keeping its routing controls open. Grouped edges also open their owning group, including nested groups. Locked groups stay closed. It starts collapsed with None selected. Click a profile to apply it immediately, or choose None to remove routing. Hovering profile cards does not change the model or viewport. Profile choices, dimensions, and expanded/collapsed state are remembered. Model-specific routing stays with the project's edges. Hovering an edge with Router shows a bold red outline. Selecting a routed edge highlights its original path and two original endpoints while keeping the machined surface visible; generated profile edges do not add selection handles. Open lines drawn on a solid face can be routed directly; they do not need to enclose a filled face. Surface profiles are centered on the line and use width and depth (or the bevel angle), while existing corner profiles keep their side-edge behavior.

Drop a section near the inside edge of an area to split it. Drag a divider to resize its areas, or double-click it to combine the sections on both sides into one area. BenchUp saves the layout immediately and keeps the sections in order with their previous disclosure settings.

The app remembers the active tool, tool-menu choices, polygon sides, Move copy mode, axis lock, snapping options, Focus on Edited Group, shortcut search, cutting-plan scope and stock settings, screenshot settings, and all Display options across reloads and app restarts. The left and right sidebars remember their scroll positions, including the floating inspector. The Outliner remembers horizontal tree scrolling and each nested geometry list's vertical scroll position. The sidebar also remembers its visibility, width, section order, expanded/collapsed sections (including nested groups and the Outliner tree), searches, and paint settings. Changes are saved immediately in local application preferences. New projects inherit the current Display and snapping options; opening an existing project uses that project's saved display and snapping settings while keeping the Focus on Edited Group preference. **Reset display** also updates the remembered preferences.

Components lists each component definition currently used in the model, including nested components, with its instance count. Search by name, choose **Place instance**, and click in the viewport to place a linked copy. Escape cancels placement. **Make Component…** creates one from the selection.

Tags supports adding, renaming, coloring, assigning, hiding, and deleting tags. Select entities and choose a tag from the assignment dropdown; **Untagged** clears the assignment. A group's or component's tag visibility applies to its descendants. Deleting a tag keeps its geometry. Tag edits support undo and redo.

Display controls axes, inference guides, grid, dimensions, hidden geometry and objects, face styles, X-Ray, color by tag, edge styles, section cuts and fill, face shadows, fog, and environment colors. Sections use a horizontal plane with an adjustable height; shadows use adjustable sun direction, elevation, and darkness rather than geolocation or calendar simulation. Shadows fall only on model faces, including vertical and tilted surfaces, and respect face holes and section cuts. Display settings and tags are saved in `.bup` and `.ketchup` projects and local autosave. **Reset display** restores the defaults.

Time & Cost includes a Router subsection with the router-bit icon. It lists saved routing paths by profile, their total length, and active work time across the whole project. The editable planning defaults are 5 minutes of setup per profile and 1 minute per meter; adjust for material, depth, and passes. Rates and the subsection's disclosure state save immediately. Routing adds active work to the time totals and exported estimate, with no drying time. None profiles are excluded.
